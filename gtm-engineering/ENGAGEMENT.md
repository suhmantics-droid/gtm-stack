# GTM-E Engagement Model

How to package "walk into a business and stand up GTM plays" as a repeatable engagement - the
deliverables, the shape, and the commercial frame. This is the wrapper around the audit → play →
freeze flow so it can be *sold and delivered*, not just *done*.

> This is a starting frame, not fixed pricing. Set actual numbers per deal. Sagar owns the commercials.

---

## 1. The offer in one line

> "I'll find the buying signals your competitors aren't looking at, build you 1-2 working outbound
> plays on them, and leave you a one-command tool to re-run them - in weeks, not a headcount."

## 2. The three engagement tiers

| Tier | Name | Scope | Deliverable | Rough shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Audit** | `/gtm-audit` only | Written audit + ranked play roadmap + client brief | Fixed fee, ~days. Low-risk entry, qualifies the bigger build. |
| 2 | **Build** | Audit + scaffold & test 1-2 plays (`/gtm-play`) | Live play(s) writing to their data home + a measurement sheet + a scorecard | Project fee, ~2-4 wks. The core. |
| 3 | **Operate / Retainer** | Run plays on a cadence, add new ones, freeze winners as skills | Monthly signal runs, scorecards, a growing library of `<client>-<play>` skills | Monthly retainer. The compounding asset. |

Land on Tier 1 → expand to 2 → retain on 3. Same flow, escalating commitment.

## 3. Deliverables checklist (what the client actually receives)

- **Client brief** (`clients/<name>.md`) - their ICP, signals, roadmap, guardrails. Theirs to keep.
- **Play roadmap** - ranked, with the quick-win + structural pick called out.
- **Live play(s)** - wired signal → enrich → score → action, writing to *their* data home.
- **Measurement sheet + scorecard** - the kill/scale evidence (MEASUREMENT.md).
- **Skill(s)** - `<client>-<play>` one-command tools they (or you) re-run.
- **Run docs** - exact commands so a play runs cold without you.

## 4. Two-week build sprint (the default Tier-2 shape)

GTM-E runs like product - sprint-based (PLAYBOOK §1). One build sprint:

- **Day 1-2:** Intake + `/gtm-audit` → client brief + roadmap. Agree the 2 plays.
- **Day 3-6:** `/gtm-play` scaffold + single-record test for each. Cost-gate before any batch.
- **Day 7-9:** Run the batch (within caps), draft the actions, set up the measurement sheet.
- **Day 10:** First reconcile + scorecard. Decide scale/iterate/kill.
- **Wrap:** Freeze any winner via `/gtm-skill-builder`. Book the next sprint or move to retainer.

## 5. What makes this defensible (why not just buy Clay)

- **Signals competitors ignore** (wallet-alpha thinking) - the edge is the play, not the tool.
- **Verified-data-first** - no guessed data, no NXDOMAIN-riddled lists. Quality over volume.
- **Lean stack** - no Clay/ZoomInfo subscription to justify; cost-controlled, free-source-first.
- **Leaves an asset** - the client keeps working skills, not a dependency on your seat.

The trade-off to be honest about up front: this is **not** a million-CRM-rows, full-send-infra
operation (see PLAYBOOK §6a, boundaries). It's surgical, signal-led, human-in-the-loop outbound.
That's the right fit for cost-sensitive SMB/mid-market; name it so expectations are set.

## 6. Qualifying the client (don't sell GTM-E to a bad fit)

See PLAYBOOK §5a. Walk away / down-scope if: TAM is tiny and local, no repeatable ICP, no system
of record and unwilling to start one (stuck at Rung 1 with no appetite to fix it), or the buyer
can't be reached by email/LinkedIn at all. Better a clean "not a fit" than a dead retainer.
