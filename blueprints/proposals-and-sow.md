# Blueprint 19 - Proposals & SOW

## What it does
Turns interest into a signed engagement: a proposal that frames the value and a statement of work
that scopes it. Extends the pricing/tiers in `../gtm-engineering/ENGAGEMENT.md` into the documents
you actually send.

## The three engagement shapes (from ENGAGEMENT.md)
- **Tier 1 - Audit:** fixed fee, days. The low-risk door-opener that qualifies the build.
- **Tier 2 - Build:** project fee, ~2-4 weeks. 1-2 live plays + measurement + a frozen skill.
- **Tier 3 - Operate:** monthly retainer. Recurring runs, scorecards, a growing skill library.

## Proposal skeleton
1. **Their problem** in their words (from discovery) - not your tooling.
2. **The outcome** you'll deliver (meetings/pipeline framing; benchmarks as industry context, not promises).
3. **Scope** - which plays, which signals, what's explicitly out (no bulk send, etc.).
4. **Deliverables** - brief, live play(s), measurement sheet, skill(s), run docs.
5. **Price + terms** - tier, month-to-month after a short initial commitment, no 12-month lock-in.
6. **Proof** - a relevant case study (Blueprint 21), once you have one.

## SOW skeleton
- Scope of work (plays + signals, explicit in/out) · Deliverables · Timeline/sprint ·
  Price + payment schedule · Data/access responsibilities (whose keys, whose lawful basis) ·
  Tooling costs (passed through or included - state which) · Success criteria (the kill/scale thresholds) ·
  Term + exit.

## How it fits (what we did vs didn't)
- We built the **pricing frame and tiers** (ENGAGEMENT.md) but did **not** run a live client
  engagement, so these are templates to adapt, not battle-tested contracts. Have a real contract
  reviewed before signing.

## Gotchas / hard rules
- **Scope the boundaries explicitly** - no bulk auto-send, not a million-row op - so expectations are set.
- **State tooling costs clearly** - "is Clay/urlscan included or passed through?" Avoid the hidden-stack surprise.
- **No 12-month lock-ins** - month-to-month after a short initial commitment; if you're good they stay.
- Benchmarks are **framing, not guarantees** - never promise a specific result in writing.

## Cost
Free (templates). Get a real SOW/contract legally reviewed once before first use.
